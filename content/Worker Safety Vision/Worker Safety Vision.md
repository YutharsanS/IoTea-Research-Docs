A system to **assist** (Industry 5.0) in worker's **safety** (SDG goals) while working on risky industrial environments.

Target Sectors: Apparel Manufacturing, Construction, Chemical Processing

- Apparel workers routinely suffer from **Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI)** due to prolonged poor posture.
- In construction and chemical industries, **unsafe zone entry** and **missing Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)** remain major causes of accidents.
- Need **preventive** safety measures.

## Description of System
---
Focuses on relaying pre-emptive safety warnings for workers *without identifying each individual*.

Proposed components would be
- MCU - Overall connection
- Low fps cam - For missing PPE
- LED indicator panels
- Haptic wristbands - For RSI
- Machine relay control - Safety cut-off
- AI - Identifying postures (Need research on this) and object detection

The system possibly shouldn't stream and store videos in cloud, since its main purpose is to give accurate warnings.

Is profitable for factories because of:
- Reduced insurance premiums
- Lower worker turnover
- Fewer medical claims

## Key Concerns
---
- Cost for customizing
- Need precise survey on the dangers of factories
- ==Fear of worker surveillance misuse (privacy)==
