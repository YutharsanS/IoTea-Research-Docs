## What Makes a Successful Business

The business should operate in a specific domain, usually the domain is some kind of a problem need to be solved.

There are three edges when looking at successful solutions, **the ideas are cheap, but execution is scarce** 

![[three_pillars_of_a_good_product.png]]

**Domain Edge**: Having a fundamental and deep understanding of a problem domain, most of the times successful solution originate from the people experienced the problem themselves and decided to build a solution for themselves. 

**Technical Edge**: Having a deep expertise on a particular technology. Usually aligning the problem in a way to utilize these expertise to the maximum extent makes some solutions out of league compared to the competition.

**Product Edge**: Having a well developed and engineered product in the same domain that's easier to be tailored to suit a new requirement. 

>[!important] Insight
>A successful solution/idea should have at least one of the aspect, but **domain edge appears to be more common in real life**
