# Reddit 
"Anyway, a little bit of internet reading pointed me in the right direction and this may help a handful of you (cause everyone complains about wifi speeds here). Anyway, most routers are automatically configured to 1 channel. Most routers in the area around you will also automatically be configured to this channel, which overloads your speeds and slows it down. So all you gotta do is change the channel! You can do this by going to your router's login (I called SLT to get my login details) and 5 mins later I changed my wifi channel from 11 to 6 and my speed immediately jumped from 970kbps to 3.4mbps. Everything works so much better now!!!"
