- Noise cancellation is made possible by an effect known as phase cancellation. In order to cancel out a sound wave, another sound wave is created in its equal but opposite form.
- Two main noise cancellation technologies,
	- **Active** --->Achieved by sending out sound waves to counteract incoming, unwanted sound,
		- Audio tech brand Ultimate Ears [estimates](https://custom.ultimateears.com/blogs/custom-made/passive-noise-isolation-vs-active-noise-cancellation) that active noise cancellation  can cut ambient noise by 30 decibels on average. 
		- Low-frequency, repetitive noise, such as lawnmowers, subway traffic and the constant murmur of a jet engine, can be reduced by much as 60 decibels.
	- **Passive** ---> Blocking or absorbing sound using physical means.
