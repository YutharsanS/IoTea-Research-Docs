**[Black Tea](https://www.google.com/search?q=Black+Tea&oq=which+type+of+teA+produced+in+sri+lanka&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUyBggAEEUYOTIHCAEQIRifBTIHCAIQIRifBTIHCAMQIRifBdIBCTE4MjgyajBqN6gCALACAA&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8&ved=2ahUKEwiXpPC7-KCSAxU6T2wGHdcWMKUQgK4QegQIAxAE):** The most popular, known for brisk, full flavors with citrus notes, categorized by region (Uva, Dimbula, etc.). 

**[Green Tea](https://www.google.com/search?q=Green+Tea&oq=which+type+of+teA+produced+in+sri+lanka&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUyBggAEEUYOTIHCAEQIRifBTIHCAIQIRifBTIHCAMQIRifBdIBCTE4MjgyajBqN6gCALACAA&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8&ved=2ahUKEwiXpPC7-KCSAxU6T2wGHdcWMKUQgK4QegQIAxAJ):** Less fermented, resulting in a yellowish-green color. - unoxidized

**[White Tea](https://www.google.com/search?q=White+Tea&oq=which+type+of+teA+produced+in+sri+lanka&gs_lcrp=EgZjaHJvbWUyBggAEEUYOTIHCAEQIRifBTIHCAIQIRifBTIHCAMQIRifBdIBCTE4MjgyajBqN6gCALACAA&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8&ved=2ahUKEwiXpPC7-KCSAxU6T2wGHdcWMKUQgK4QegQIAxAO):** Least processed, often "Silver Tips," made from buds and sun-dried, making it very fine and expensive. - least processed

| Tea Type                | Oxidation Level             | Main Raw Material        | Processing Method Used                 | Process Explanation (brief)                                                                         | Notes                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Black Tea               | Fully oxidized              | Mature tea leaves        | Orthodox / CTC                         | Leaves are withered, rolled (or cut–tear–curl), fully oxidized, fired, graded                       | Dominant tea type in Sri Lanka (≈95% of production). Regional flavors: Uva, Dimbula, Nuwara Eliya |
| Green Tea               | Unoxidized                  | Young leaves             | Modified Orthodox (no oxidation)       | Leaves are steamed or pan-fired immediately after plucking to stop oxidation, then rolled and dried | Sri Lanka produces pan-fired & steamed green teas mainly for export                               |
| White Tea               | Minimal / natural oxidation | Tender buds only         | Sun-drying (handcrafted)<br>No rolling | **Buds** are carefully plucked, withered naturally under sunlight or controlled shade, then dried   | Includes _Silver Tips_ and _Golden Tips_; very expensive, low volume                              |
| Oolong Tea              | Partially oxidized (10–70%) | Young leaves             | Controlled partial oxidation           | Leaves are withered, gently bruised, partially oxidized, then fired to stop oxidation               | Produced in very small quantities in Sri Lanka                                                    |
| Purple Tea              | Low oxidation (varies)      | Purple-leaf **cultivar** | Green / Black style processing         | Process depends on final product: either oxidation stopped early or allowed fully                   | High antioxidant content; niche export product                                                    |
| Herbal / Specialty Teas | None                        | Non-tea plants           | Drying / infusion                      | Not true tea (no _Camellia sinensis_)                                                               | Not classified as Ceylon Tea legally                                                              |

- For Purple Tea :
	- Green-style → oxidation stopped early
	- Black-style → full oxidation allowed
	Unique factor:
	- Purple leaves contain **anthocyanins**
	- Higher antioxidant content than regular tea
