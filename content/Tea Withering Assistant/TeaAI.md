## Market Intelligence and Analytics (TeaMate)
---

The **TeaMate** suite provides a comprehensive visualization of the tea landscape to help traders make informed buying and selling decisions.

• **Competitor Analysis:** Tracks market trends in tea quantity and pricing, analyzes competitor behavior, and identifies market share and growth opportunities.

• **Weather Analytics:** Offers detailed weather maps and dynamic charts for specific factories, allowing users to track environmental changes over time.

• **Price History and Mapping:** Enables traders to track the prices of specific tea grades in various regions or garden marks. It also includes a **Price Heat Map** specifically for Sri Lankan tea prices to show how hotspots shift geographically over time.

• **Trade Resources:** Provides centralized access to key exchange rates and allows users to download publicly released Weekly Broker Market Reports.

## AI-Powered Operational Tools
---

TeaAI uses specialized AI to automate traditionally manual processes like tasting and grading:

• **TeaTalk (Voice Recognition):** This tool uses voice-to-text technology specifically trained on **tea industry terminology**. It allows tasters to transcribe notes—such as "Wiry, Little Gray" or "Some Stalks"—directly into a digital format.

• **TeaRetina (Visual Grading):** This system uses AI to **digitize tea standards**. A "Game Changer" feature of this tool is its ability to match these scanned standards against **over 10,000 weekly auction samples** with a single click, ensuring consistent quality control.

## Data Privacy and Security (TeaFence)
---

The website emphasizes an "Industry Leading" privacy architecture called **TeaFence**.

• **TeaFence** acts as an impenetrable firewall, ensuring that a customer’s sensitive data never leaves their secure environment.

• The system is designed to process information **without TeaAI having access to the raw data** or the user's specific quality standards.

• It includes **comprehensive user management**, allowing organizations to create individual accounts with role-based access and permissions.
