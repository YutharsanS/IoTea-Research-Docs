- VPD is the difference between **saturation** vapor pressure and the **actual amount of water vapor** currently in the air.
	i.e., how strong the moisture content should be removed by air.

- Low VPD = Air is humid (Low temp and High RH) = Moisture removal is slow
	Microbial growth

- Moderate = **Required**

- High VPD = Moisture removal is too fast
	Case hardening


> Basically VPD is combining temperature and RH in one calculation.
