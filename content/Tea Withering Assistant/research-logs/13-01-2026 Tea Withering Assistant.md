
The paper summary [[@Wang2025|Predicting the Degree of Fresh Tea Leaves Withering Using Image Classification Confidence]] suggests the complete development of a detection model from the data acquisition and model selection and model training. But the paper is more focused on the computer vision based approach to solve the withering detection issue. **the results look impressive**.

Before moving into how to implementation solution, it would be good to focus on the what to implement first, that's where this journal article comes in:  [[@Ayesha2023|Revolutionizing Sri Lankan Tea Industry:A Comprehensive Analysis of the Economic Viability of Implementing IoT Applications]] , upshot of the paper is, efficiencies that can be gained from implementing an IoT solution is mainly in the sectors of *labor cost* and *transport*. We should be really careful about **empowering the worker part** since factories maybe into cutting down labor counts which is opposite of what were are trying to achieve.
