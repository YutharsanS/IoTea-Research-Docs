## Market Intelligence and Analytics (TeaMate)
---
• **Competitor Analysis:** Tracks market trends in tea quantity and pricing, analyzes competitor behavior, and identifies market share and growth opportunities.

• **Weather Analytics:** Offers detailed weather maps and dynamic charts for specific factories, allowing users to track environmental changes over time.

• **Price History and Mapping:** Enables traders to track the prices of specific tea grades in various regions or garden marks. It also includes a **Price Heat Map** specifically for Sri Lankan tea prices to show how hotspots shift geographically over time.

• **Trade Resources:** Provides centralized access to key exchange rates and allows users to download publicly released Weekly Broker Market Reports.


## AI-Powered Operational Tools
---
• **TeaTalk (Voice Recognition):** Uses voice-to-text technology specifically trained on tea industry terminology. It allows tasters to transcribe notes—such as "Wiry, Little Gray" or "Some Stalks"—directly into a digital format.

• **TeaRetina (Visual Grading):** Match  scanned standards against over 10,000 weekly auction samples, ensuring consistent quality control.


## Data Privacy and Security (TeaFence)
---
• TeaFence ensures that a customer’s sensitive data never leaves their secure environment.

• The system is designed to process information without TeaAI having access to the raw data or the user's specific quality standards.

• Comprehensive user management, allowing organizations to create individual accounts with role-based access and permissions.

# Major Audience
Major audiences for `Teaai.ai` and their operations are the people who distribute and selling tea to consumers.
